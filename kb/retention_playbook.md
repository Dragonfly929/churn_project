# Retention playbook (simulated bank, internal)

## Silence — the client stopped talking and transacting
Signals: no messages for 6 months, survey not answered, days since last transaction rising, silent decline flag.
Recommended action: a personal call from the client's consultant within 3 working days. Do not send automated offers first: silent clients ignore mass e-mails. Ask an open question about needs; offer a free account review.
Offer code: personal_call. Cost about 25 EUR (consultant time).

## Dissatisfaction — complaints and negative messages
Signals: complaints in the last 180 days, negative sentiment, low NPS score.
Recommended action: resolve the open complaint first, then apologise and offer a fee waiver for 6 months. The complaint must be closed before any commercial offer.
Offer code: fee_waiver. Cost about 40 EUR.

## Money withdrawal — balance drained, large outgoing transfers
Signals: negative balance change over 3 months, high outgoing amounts, salary no longer received.
Recommended action: a deposit bonus (higher interest for 12 months) and a check whether a competitor offered better conditions.
Offer code: deposit_bonus. Cost about 80 EUR.

## Disengagement — gradual fade of activity
Signals: falling transaction trend, inactive member, fewer digital sessions.
Recommended action: card cashback campaign for 3 months and a reminder of unused app features.
Offer code: card_cashback. Cost about 50 EUR.

## Capacity rule
The retention team handles at most the daily capacity set in the control panel; alerts are handled highest risk first. Alerts above capacity wait for the next day.
