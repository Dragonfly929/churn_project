# Ethics and compliance policy for automated retention

## Non-discrimination
Retention offers must never depend on gender, age or country. The offer is chosen from the reason of the alert only. Fairness gaps in recall between groups are measured every period and limited in the contract matrix.

## Human in the loop (GDPR Art. 22)
The model only proposes. An employee can override or cancel any automatic offer. No decision with legal or similarly significant effect is made only by the machine.

## Right to object to direct marketing (GDPR Art. 21)
Clients with the marketing opt-out flag receive no commercial offer. For them only a service call about an open complaint is allowed.

## Data minimisation
Names and contact data are not stored in the analytical warehouse. Analysts see features, not identities.

## Transparency
Every alert carries an explanation (SHAP reason and the main features). The client can ask why he was contacted.
