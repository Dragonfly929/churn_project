Plus 1.0 percentage point interest on a term deposit for 12 months. Suitable when money is being moved to another bank.

2 percent cashback on card payments for 3 months. Suitable for clients whose card activity is fading.

A call from the dedicated consultant, with a free review of the client's needs and products. Suitable for silent clients who do not react to automated messages.
